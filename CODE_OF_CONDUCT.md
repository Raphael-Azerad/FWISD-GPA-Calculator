# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project is for students, families, and contributors who want GPA and grade math to be easier to understand. Be respectful, patient, and specific.

## Not Okay

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Sharing private student information
- Presenting unofficial estimates as official FWISD decisions
- Pressuring students to disclose grades or rank data

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who ignore these expectations.
